Welcome to UseGalaxy Canada
The main Canadian public Galaxy server
Galaxy is an open source web-based platform for accessible, reproducible and transparent computational research and data intensive analysis. It is used by researchers across the globe on free public infrastructure.

To fully profit from the UseGalaxy.ca platform and run your analysis you will need to login using the link on the upper right corner. Once on the login page select your institution or Google authentication to login (see our Data Policies page for the implications of your authentication choice).
