GenAP IS NOW RETIRED

After serving the Canadian Life Science research community for a decade, the Genomics and Genomics Analysis Platform (GenAP) has retired on May 1st, 2025. To ensure continuity of your research, we recommend the following alternatives:


    Alternative to Galaxy: UseGalaxy Canada
    Alternatives to Datahubs: Globus Collections
    Alternatives to FileBrowsers: Globus Collections

For questions or assistance, please reach out through our Contact Form
